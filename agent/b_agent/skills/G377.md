Require Internal Verification of Comparison Rules

**When to apply**: When the correctness of the solution hinges on a specific comparison rule (case-sensitive/insensitive, locale-aware sorting, custom comparator, tolerance for floating-point numbers).
**What to do**: Instruct the model to include a simple internal verification, such as a comment explicitly stating the rule or an `assert` statement that demonstrates it. This acts as a "check your work" step for the model.
**Example**: "In your implementation, include a comment like `# Comparison is case-insensitive` next to the key logic. Additionally, add a simple assert for clarity: `assert compare("Text", "text") is True`."
**Why it works**: This technique forces the model to concretely articulate the rule during code generation, reducing the chance of an implicit, incorrect assumption. The act of writing the verification reinforces the constraint in the model's reasoning process, leading to more accurate code.
